About Us

Welcome to TestforUHS

TestforUHS is an interactive digital study platform designed specifically for medical students preparing for academic examinations and national licensing tests.

Key Features

  • Study Mode: Instant feedback with auto-scrolling to reinforce active recall.
  • Timed Quiz Mode: Simulated exam conditions with countdown timers and automated scoring.
  • Vault Mastery System: Smart tracking that saves missed questions until answered correctly twice in a row.
  • Anki Flashcard Exporter: One-click export of missed questions to Anki deck format (`.txt`).

Our Mission

To provide free, fast, and reliable medical study resources to help students excel in their studies and clinical training.
